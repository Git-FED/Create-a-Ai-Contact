# 01 — Concept

## What is AI-Contact?

AI-Contact is a recognizable, dedicated contact identity for an AI assistant. Instead of hiding the assistant behind a generic form or presenting it as a human employee, the identity makes the AI's role explicit while giving people a familiar email-based way to interact with it.

## Design principles

1. **Clear identity:** Messages should say that the correspondent is an AI system.
2. **Useful first response:** The assistant should acknowledge the request, state what it can do, and ask only for information that is necessary.
3. **Bounded autonomy:** High-impact actions, sensitive data handling, and uncertain requests require escalation.
4. **Traceability:** Inbound messages, decisions, and outbound replies should be logged according to the applicable retention policy.
5. **Human fallback:** Every contact path should provide a human escalation route.

## Suggested identity

- Display name: `AI Contact`
- Address: `ai@your-domain.example`
- Signature: `AI Contact — an AI assistant for Your Organization`

The exact name and address can be changed to fit the organization, but the AI nature of the identity should remain visible.
